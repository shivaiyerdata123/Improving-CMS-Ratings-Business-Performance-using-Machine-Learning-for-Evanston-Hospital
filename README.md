# Improving-CMS-Ratings-Business-Performance-using-Machine-Learning-for-Evanston-Hospital
Evanston Hospital has been stuck at a 3-star CMS rating for 5 years, impacting revenue and insurer preference. We need to understand the root cause underlying the low CMS ratings and provide alternatives to boost ratings and revenue.

Evanston Hospital faces 7% quarterly net profit decline over 6 quarters and 5% annual drop in patients/insurers for 5 years.

Regional shift to private healthcare favors competitors over Evanston due to its average CMS rating of 3.

Major investments in equipment, staff, lab, and NICU failed to improve ratings.

Root cause: Management lacks data-driven insights on rating-impacting services; decisions relied on guesswork.
